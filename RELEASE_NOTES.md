# Version 0.1.0: Paper-associated software and selected numerical results

Accompanies Paper I, Foundations and Full-Vector Validation, and Paper II, Three-Dimensional Potential-Flow Reconstruction from Radial Velocities.

This release includes 22 selected research sources, 174 saved numerical/configuration records, all 47 figure/table mapping entries, a synthetic component demo, saved-capacity recomputation, a provenance-linked table-review export and a saved-data redraw example. Four software authors are credited in the confirmed order: Tsutomu T. Takeuchi, Gayathri Asok, Hai-Xia Ma, and Yu Ogane.

The scientific sources and stored records are unchanged from the locally checked 0.1.0rc1 snapshot. Code/documentation: BSD-3-Clause. Saved numerical records and their source-provenance metadata: CC BY 4.0. The licenses apply to separate components.

The original cosmological catalogs, snapshots and large caches are omitted. The archive does not assert full one-command reproduction of all high-mode analyses, all final figures, or validated applicability to a real peculiar-velocity survey. See docs/reproducibility.md and docs/data_scope.md.

Upload the exact same final ZIP to this GitHub release and to its single Zenodo software record. Cite the version DOI from that record in both papers. A draft or reserved DOI is not a published archive.
